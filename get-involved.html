FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to be Asian to join?

Nope! ASA if for anyone who want to hang out, celebrate culture, and have fun.

Is this just a cultural club?

ASA is a place to hang out with your friends, build community, eat food, celebrate traditions, fundraise for real causes.

Who runs Mayo ASA?

Wilson Wang, Chen Liang, Kevin Wang, Nathaniel Yuan, and Raunak Scheuermann are the club orginizers. Email them at hello@mayoasa.com if you have any questions.

Check out the About page if you want the full story.

Does Mayo ASA do charity work?

Yes! Right now we're raising money for Nepal flood relief, to help communities recovering from the August flash floods. Check out the Global Impact page for how to help.

Can my club do a joint event with Mayo ASA?

Of course! We would love to partner with other clubs. Reach out to us at hello@mayoasa.com to plan a event or fundraiser together.
