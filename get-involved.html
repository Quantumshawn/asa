FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to be Asian to join?

No. Mayo ASA is a community for Asian American, Pacific Islander, and Desi students, and for the allies who want to learn, build, and advocate alongside them.

Is this just a cultural club?

No — culture is part of the community pillar, but education and advocacy carry equal weight. Expect discussions on history and current issues, and real work on problems at Mayo, not just social events.

Is Mayo ASA against any other club or group?

No. Mayo ASA is built to collaborate, not compete — see our Collaborate page for the organizations we're hoping to work with.

Who runs Mayo ASA?

The club was founded by Wilson Wang, along with Chen Liang, Kevin Wang, and Nathaniel Yuan. See the About page for more on the mission behind it.

Does Mayo ASA do charity work?

Yes — alongside campus advocacy, we're running a fundraising initiative for Nepal flood relief, supporting communities recovering from the August 2026 flash floods. See the Global Impact page for the plan and how to help.

How can my club plan a joint event with Mayo ASA?

Reach out using the contact info above — we're actively looking for coalition partners and already have a running list of event ideas on the Collaborate page.

Show up once. See if it's for you.
