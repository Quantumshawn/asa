FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to be Asian to join?

Nope! Mayo ASA is for Asian American, Pacific Islander, and Desi students, and for any allies who want to hang out, celebrate culture, and give back with us.

Is this just a cultural club?

Pretty much, yeah. Plus we fundraise for real causes. It's community, food, traditions, celebrations, and right now, raising money for Nepal flood relief too. That's really it, no hidden agenda.

Who runs Mayo ASA?

Wilson Wang started it, along with Chen Liang, Kevin Wang, and Nathaniel Yuan. Check out the About page if you want the full story.

Does Mayo ASA do charity work?

Yep! Right now we're raising money for Nepal flood relief, to help communities recovering from the August 2026 flash floods. Check out the Global Impact page for how to help.

Can my club do a joint event with Mayo ASA?

Sure. Reach out using the contact info above if you want to plan a joint cultural event or fundraiser together.
