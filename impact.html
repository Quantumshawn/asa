Global Impact

Advocacy that reaches home—and abroad.

Our advocacy pillar isn't limited to Mayo's hallways. We're raising funds for Nepal flood relief, supporting communities recovering from the flash floods that hit Nepal in August 2026.

Why this exists

The same advocacy, a bigger stage

Identifying a real problem, gathering evidence, finding the right partners, and following through until something changes — that's the advocacy pillar at Mayo. Global Impact is that exact process, aimed at a problem that reaches further than our campus: communities in Nepal recovering from the catastrophic flash floods that hit the Nepal–Tibet border region in August 2026, displacing tens of thousands of people.

Why Nepal

A starting point, not a limit

In August 2026, catastrophic flash floods tore through Nepal's border region with Tibet, displacing tens of thousands of people and damaging critical infrastructure. We're starting here because the need is immediate — but as Mayo ASA grows, we want Global Impact to grow with it, toward other communities that need the same kind of concrete support.
