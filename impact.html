Global Impact

Community here. Impact there.

Mayo ASA isn't just hanging out and eating good food (though there's plenty of that too). We're also raising money for Nepal flood relief. Real support for people recovering from the flash floods that hit in August 2026.

Why this exists

Why we're doing this

Building community and celebrating culture matters a lot to us. But we also wanted to actually do something, not just talk about it. That's Global Impact: real fundraising for a real problem, for communities in Nepal recovering from the flash floods that hit the Nepal–Tibet border region in August 2026 and displaced tens of thousands of people.

Why Nepal

A starting point, not a limit

In August 2026, massive flash floods tore through Nepal's border region with Tibet, displacing tens of thousands of people. We're starting here because the need is real and it's happening right now. But as Mayo ASA grows, we want this to grow with it, toward other places that need the same kind of help.
