Join Mayo ASA

Mayo High School · Student-Founded, Student-Run

One community.
Built on culture.

We're a community for Asian American students at Mayo. We serve good food, share our culture, and give everyone a chance to help people who need it.

Join Mayo ASA Meet the founders

A lot of us are looking for a real sense of belonging and a way to connect over where we come from, especially since Mayo doesn't offer Asian language classes. We've got some great clubs here, but ASA makes a space for Asian students to celebrate who we are, and a way to actually help solve real problems, like raising money for disaster relief overseas.

Wilson Wang, Founder
What we're about

It's about community and culture.

Mayo ASA is a space to hang out, make friends, and have fun. We have food, traditions, celebrations, all of it.

We will also help people in need. Such as organizing fundraisers for disaster relief.
