Collaboration

Coalition, not competition.

Mayo ASA is explicitly not built to be "anti" anyone. Other identity-based and equity-focused clubs aren't rivals for attention — they're the organizations best positioned to build with us.

Why

Different experiences, shared work

Minority groups in America don't experience stereotyping, discrimination, or history the same way — and pretending otherwise flattens everyone's story. But there's real overlap in the work: representation, civil rights history, immigration and identity, and pushing institutions to do better. That overlap is where coalition events live.
