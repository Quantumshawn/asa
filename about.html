About Mayo ASA

A community built on culture, food, and having fun.

Here's what Mayo ASA is really about.

Mission

Our mission

We want Mayo ASA to be a real community for Asian American, Pacific Islander, and Desi students, built around culture and connection, with room to actually help people along the way.

A lot of us are looking for a real sense of belonging and a way to connect over where we come from, especially since our school doesn't even offer Asian language or cultural classes. We've already got some great culture clubs here, but ASA fills in what they don't: a space for Asian students and allies to celebrate who we are, and a way to actually do something about real problems too, like raising money for disaster relief overseas.

Wilson Wang, Founder
What we're about

Culture and community, first

Wilson Wang, Chen Liang, Kevin Wang, and Nathaniel Yuan started Mayo ASA because we wanted Asian students at Mayo to have a real space of their own with regular meetings, celebrations, food, and traditions.

You don't have to be Asian to join, everyone is welcome.

Beyond Mayo

Giving back doesn't stop at Mayo

We're running a fundraiser for Nepal flood relief, to help people recovering from the flash floods that hit Nepal in August.

Check out our Global Impact page for the full details and how to help.
